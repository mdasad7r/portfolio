Mohammed Asad

Computer Vision Researcher

Graduate @ DTU, India

I love exploring how machines learn to see the beautiful world around us.

I am currently a Researcher at CRCV, University of Central Florida, exploring object-centric visual learning, and a Research Intern at MIAL Lab, Simon Fraser University, where I work on computational methods for biological and medical imaging, including graph-based spatial analysis. Also working with Dr. Chhavi Dhiman on face anti-spoofing for robust biometric security.

Previously, at MMDA Lab, DTU, I worked on gait recognition for surveillance under variations in viewpoint, clothing, carrying, and noise. At CALIBRE-DTU, I worked on mammography-based breast cancer classification with Dr. Sudhir Singh and Dr. Rahul Kataria, followed by my bachelor's thesis under the supervision of Dr. AK Gautam on robust apple detection in challenging orchard environments, which earned an A+ and was published at ICICV 2026. I also worked as a Computer Vision Intern at DeepSight AI Labs, building practical AI systems.

I am now preparing to pursue a PhD in visual intelligence, with the goal of developing vision systems that are robust, adaptable, and useful in the real world.

Gait Recognition with Temporal Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks

Mohammed Asad, Dr. Dinesh Kumar Vishwakarma

A compact gait-recognition model that learns short-term walking cycles and longer motion context, improving silhouette-based identity matching when faces, clothing, views, and video conditions are unreliable.

Mohammed Asad, Dr. Ajai Kumar Gautam, Priyanshu Dhiman, Rishi Raj Prajapati
