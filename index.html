Mohammed Asad

Computer Vision Researcher

Graduate @ DTU, India

I study how machines perceive, structure, and reason about the visual world.

I am currently a Researcher at the University of Central Florida, where my work focuses on vision-language models and visual intelligence.

Previously, I worked on gait recognition for surveillance applications with Dr. DKV, where faces are often unreliable and walking patterns can become a stronger identity cue. That experience drew me toward visual recognition problems where AI must operate under noise, occlusion, limited data, and real deployment constraints. I later explored similar challenges in mammography-based breast cancer classification at CALIBRE-DTU and orchard-scale apple detection and deployment with Dr. AK Gautam, where models must interpret complex visual patterns rather than merely perform well on clean benchmarks. I also worked as a Computer Vision Intern at DeepSight AI Labs, where I gained industry experience translating visual AI research into practical, deployable systems.

My long-term goal is to pursue a PhD at a leading research university and contribute to reliable, interpretable, and impactful visual AI systems.
